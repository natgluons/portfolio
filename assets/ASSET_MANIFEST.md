# Asset Manifest

## Theme-aware icons and artwork

Shared site artwork and icons are grouped by theme:

- `icons/light/` - light-mode decorations, Experience section icons, and `intro-*.svg` artwork
- `icons/dark/` - dark-mode decorations and `intro-*.svg` artwork
- `icons/shared/` - theme-independent mascots, Experience artwork and education logos, and shared icons

Carousel and storybook PNG images remain in `images/` and `images/intro/`.
